# Академгородок Лаборатории № 2 — interior & detail notes

Reference images live in `refs/interior/` (git-ignored); every file is listed with source, author and
licence in `refs/interior/manifest.json`. File names below are in that folder unless a URL is given.

Evidence tags:
- **[fact: source]**: stated in a text source.
- **[seen: file]**: visible in a photo I looked at.
- **[est.]**: my estimate from photos or proportions (people, doors and tiles used for scale).
- **[guess]**: typical for 1945–50 Moscow "stalinka" practice. Not confirmed for these houses.

**Plans.** I found no floor plan of a flat or a stair section; no BTI plan, listing plan or published plan.
The only drawings found are two archival sheets, photographed in m24.ru 2015:
- `m24_2015_houses_05.jpg`: 1945/46 GUAS NKVD setting-out drawing, «Разбивочный чертёж 20-ти кварт. домов корп. I и корп. II», 1:500, signed by Karlik. It shows each block divided into sections, with a hatched stair rectangle in each section. Crops are `crop_k1b/crop_k2` in the scratchpad and are not kept.
- `m24_2015_houses_06.jpg`: a 1946 street elevation of a 3-storey block.

Room sizes below therefore come from listing areas plus reasoning, and are marked **[est.]**.

## (a) Typical entrance (подъезд) and stair

**Exterior doors** (yard side; the street side has no entrances, see the 1946 elevation)
- Style: double-leaf timber door painted dark brown (≈RAL 8017), set in a flat white plaster surround about 25 cm wide. Each leaf has a glazed upper panel. Below it is a fielded panel, and one door type has a round carved boss [seen: `m24_2015_houses_07.jpg`, `commons_Marshal_Novikov_str._1_2_.jpg`, `commons_Marshal_Novikov_str._1_3_.jpg`]. A modern intercom panel is fixed on the jamb. A wall lamp on a bracket sits above the door [seen: _1_3_].
- Size: about **1.35–1.45 m wide × 2.4–2.5 m high** for the pair; each leaf is 0.65–0.72 m [est.], scaled from a person and the plinth in `m24_2015_houses_07.jpg` and `_1_2_.jpg`. One leaf alone (≈0.7 m) is **below the 1.08 m game minimum**. Both leaves open together are fine; model them as normally both open or as auto-opening.
- Step: the door sits about 0.15–0.3 m above the asphalt, with a single step or threshold slab. The plinth is grey and ≈0.4 m high [seen: _1_3_].
- On the central block, the middle entrance on the yard axis has a rusticated "rocky" stone portal with a wedge keystone. It sits under a stack of loggias with columns, with a fountain in front [seen: `commons_Marshal_Novikov_str._1_2_.jpg`, `m24_2015_houses_10.jpg`]. The two flanking doors are plain.
- The 1946 elevation (`m24_2015_houses_06.jpg`) shows round-arched rusticated portals on the street front of a side block. On the built houses these became the arched **gate pylons** with S-scroll "завитки" between the blocks (`commons_Marshal_Novikov_str._1_gates.jpg`, `aif_2019_01.jpg`), not entrance doors. Corner quoins and a "1947" cartouche are on the corners (`commons_Shchukino…54_/56_.jpg`).

**Vestibule**
- No photo. The press notes "просторные подъезды" [fact: Фочкин 2021 p.18 → `fochkin2021_p17.jpg`]. Residents removed janitor's rooms («дворницкие») from the entrances (Telegram post 34), so a small room off the ground-floor lobby existed. Basement access is from the stair [guess].
- Proposed model: a lobby about **2.6 m wide × 2.5 m deep**, raised 3–5 steps (≈0.5–0.75 m) to the first landing, with a door to the basement flight under the first flight [est./guess].

**Stair**
- Layout: a **dog-leg stair, two flights per storey**. On the yard facades a column of windows sits half a storey off the room windows, directly above each entrance door, and an oculus is at the top. These are the windows of the half-landings [seen: `commons_Marshal_Novikov_str._1_3_.jpg` (central column above the door), `commons_Maksimova_str._8_1_.jpg`]. On the 1946 setting-out drawing each stair is a separate hatched rectangle on the yard side of its section [seen: `m24_2015_houses_05.jpg`].
- Width: the listing says "2 m". I read this as **each flight ≈1.2–1.3 m clear**, in a stair cell **≈2.7–2.9 m wide** (two flights plus a 10–20 cm well) and ≈5.5–6 m deep (two 9–10-riser flights of 9 × 0.30 m plus landings ≈1.5 m) [est.]. A 2 m-wide single flight is improbable at this scale, so if the 2 m means the cell, use 2.4 m minimum.
- Risers ≈ 3.65 m / 22 ≈ **0.166 m**, treads ≈0.29–0.30 m [est.; "комфортный уклон" per listing].
- Railing: **metal balusters with a timber handrail**, which is restored and varnished in the 2025 capital-repair plan [fact: mos.ru news 174745073 / aif.ru on Максимова 8: «отремонтируют лестничные ограждения… поручни… покроют лаком»]. The baluster pattern is not photographed. Use simple vertical square bars at about 12 cm with a scroll panel every flight, a typical Zholtovsky-office motif [guess]. Handrail height 0.9 m [guess].
- Materials and colours: walls and ceilings are painted over plaster, with stucco in the "общественные интерьеры" [fact: mos.ru; Telegram post 34 "восстановление лепнины… общественных интерьеров"]. Suggested palette: a lower dado in oil paint (ochre or green-grey) to about 1.5 m, with lime-white above [guess]. Steps are probably cast terrazzo or concrete with mosaic finish, landings in small ceramic tiles (e.g. 10 × 10 cm "metlakh" tiles) [guess]. Wooden mailboxes on the ground landing [guess]. The windows have three casements with a transom, white-painted timber [seen on facades].
- The flat entrance doors on landings are not photographed. Use a one-and-a-half-leaf door, 0.9 + 0.4 m, 2.3 m high, leather-padded or panelled [guess]. The **0.9 m leaf alone is below 1.08 m**, so either open both leaves or use 1.10 m single leaves.

## (b) Typical flat

**Programme (sources)**
- 3-room flat: living-dining room, kitchen with a maid's alcove, two bedrooms with the bathroom between them. The room next to the kitchen can serve as a study [fact: Московское наследие 2007 №4 via Wikipedia].
- Listing 369087179 (4th floor of a 4-storey 1947 house, probably Novikova 1): 102.8 m² total / 70 living / kitchen 8.7–9 m². Separate WC and bath, windows to both sides ("сквозное проветривание"), no load-bearing partitions inside, gas, ceiling 3.2 m. The address is printed as «Маршала Новикова, 5», which is likely a listing error, since only house 1 has 4 floors.
- Listing 355187427 / 9289717638 (Novikova 3 or 1, ground floor): 160 m² total, 101.2 living, 4 isolated rooms facing the yard plus a study, two bathrooms, veranda 14 m², basement room 14 m², and a second entrance from the veranda. It is modernised; its 45.5 m² kitchen-living room is a merger.

**Reasoned plan of a 3-room flat [est.]**
The flat runs through the building, so its depth equals the building depth, ≈12–12.5 m between outer walls. The width per flat is ≈8.3 m (102.8 m² / 12.4 m).

```
 street side  ───────────────────────────────── 12.4 m deep ───────────────►  yard side
 ┌──────────────┬──────────────┐
 │ Living-dining│  Bedroom 1   │   ← street windows (2 + 1)
 │ 5.6 x 4.9 m  │ 4.5 x 4.0 m  │
 │  ≈27.5 m²    │   ≈18 m²     │
 ├────┬─────────┼──────┬───────┤
 │Hall│ corridor 1.4 m │ Bath  │   bath 2.4 x 1.9 between the bedrooms
 │2.6 │                │ ≈4.5  │   WC 1.5 x 0.9 separate
 ├────┴───┬────────────┼───────┤
 │Kitchen │ Room 3 /   │Bedroom│   ← yard windows
 │3.0x3.0 │ study      │  2    │
 │ +maid  │ 4.0 x 3.6  │4.5x4.4│
 │ alcove │  ≈14.5 m²  │ ≈20 m²│
 │1.8x1.4 │            │       │
 └────────┴────────────┴───────┘
                ▲ entrance from the stair landing on the yard side
```

Living ≈27.5 + 18 + 20 = 65.5 m² (14.5 m² if the study counts as a separate room). That is close to the listed 70, which implies the rooms are slightly larger. Rooms 4.0–5.6 m across, with 3.3 m ceilings, are typical.

**Windows from inside**
- Residential windows are tripartite: a three-leaf timber casement with a transom, white. Opening ≈**1.45 m wide × 2.0 m high**, sill ≈0.85 m above the floor, head ≈2.85 m (≈0.45 m below the ceiling) [est. from facades `_1_3_`, `_1_4_`, `Maksimova_8_1_`: windows ≈0.55 of the storey height]. Some windows on the yard side are paired, with a pilaster between them (`_1_4_`).
- Reveals are deep: ≈0.5–0.6 m to the glass plane, with a wide timber sill board [seen: `mk_novikova3_160m2_02.jpg` (modern PVC frame in an old opening)].
- Balcony and loggia doors: two leaves plus transom, ≈1.2 × 2.5 m [est. from `_1_2_`].
- Ground-floor bay verandas have big multi-pane glazing and an arched window (Maksimova 8: `commons_Maksimova_str._8_2_.jpg`; end of block 1: `m24_2015_houses_08.jpg`).

**Doors (historic vs. game rule ≥1.08 m clear × ≥2.10 m)**

| Door | Likely historic size | Game check |
|---|---|---|
| Living-dining ↔ hall: glazed double door | 1.20–1.30 × 2.30–2.40 m [guess] | OK |
| Bedrooms, study | single 0.80–0.90 × 2.20–2.30 m [guess] | **too narrow → widen to 1.10 m** |
| Kitchen | 0.80 × 2.20 m [guess] | **too narrow** |
| Bath, WC | 0.60–0.70 × 2.00–2.10 m [guess] | **too narrow, and low** |
| Flat entrance | 0.90 (+0.40) × 2.30 m [guess] | single leaf too narrow |
| Entrance (подъезд) pair | 1.40 × 2.45 m [est.] | OK when both leaves open |
| Museum yard exit door | glazed, ≈0.9 × 2.2 m [seen: `commons_…excursion.jpg`] | **too narrow** |

**Finishes**
- Floors: **oak parquet**, "сохранилась лепнина, есть дубовый паркет" [fact: Cian 4/4 1947 listing snippet via search]. The museum shows a floor polisher (полотёр) used to wax parquet [fact: kp.ru 2019]. The pattern is not photographed; use **herringbone (ёлочка), boards ≈7 × 40 cm**, the standard Moscow stalinka laying [guess]. Kitchen, bath and WC: small hexagonal or square ceramic tiles [guess].
- Ceilings: lime-white with a multi-step **profiled plaster cornice** ≈25–35 cm deep. Some ceilings have a moulded rectangular frame about 40 cm in from the cornice [seen: `m24_2016_flat_09.jpg`, `commons_…_07/_08/_11.jpg`, `m24_2015_museum_03.jpg`]. These photos show the former shop. In the flats, stucco survives per the listing snippet, and there is a ceiling rose under each chandelier [guess].
- Walls: wallpaper with a small pattern (cream and light-green damask) or distemper. The museum's flat hall has cream paper with a small star motif above an ivory **15 × 15 cm tile dado to ≈1.6 m**. The tiles are left from the shop, so they are not a flat finish [seen: `m24_2016_flat_04.jpg`, `commons_…_02/_03.jpg`].
- Heating: cast-iron sectional radiators under the windows (MS-140-type, ≈0.6 m tall) [guess; the listing photo shows a modern tubular radiator in the same position]. Many brick chimney stacks with decorative caps rise on the roofs [seen: `_1_3_`, `_1_4_`, `m24_2015_houses_08.jpg`]. Woodsheds stood in the yards until the late 1960s [fact: svpressa 2010]. So the kitchens probably had a solid-fuel range or water heater flue at first, later gas («газ» in the 2026 listing) [guess]. A tiled stove (печь-голландка) in rooms is **not evidenced**.
- Light fittings (museum, all period-correct props): a 5-arm chandelier with opal glass globes ⌀15 cm and a brass ring ⌀≈60 cm (`commons_…_02.jpg`); a 5-arm "tulip" chandelier with frosted glass (`commons_…_08.jpg`); a crystal pendant (`_03`); an opal cylinder pendant with a chrome band ⌀30 cm (`m24_2016_flat_04.jpg`); a desk lamp with an orange mica shade (`m24_2016_flat_05.jpg`); a green enamel desk lamp (`m24_2015_museum_06.jpg`).

## (c) Gastronom hall (ground floor of Novikova 1, now the museum)

Sources: Wikipedia; m24 2016; svpressa 2010; Фочкин 2021. Photos: `m24_2015_museum_04/05/07/08/09`, `m24_2016_shop_01–08`, `commons_…_05/_06/_09/_10/excursion`, `m24_2015_houses_09.jpg` (shop windows from outside).

- History: the shop was №22 «Бакалея», and from 1951 «Гастроном № 7». It was supplied "по высшему разряду" and was partly closed to outsiders («полузакрытый»). A hairdresser's was next door. It closed in the early 2000s [fact: Wikipedia, svpressa, mos.ru PDF].
- Now two connected halls joined by a **round arch ≈2.4 m wide, ≈3.0 m to the crown** [est.; seen: `m24_2016_shop_03.jpg`]:
  1. The **"Гастроном" hall**, with light-blue/white glazed square tiles **≈15 × 15 cm** and dark grout up to **≈2.3 m**, painted plaster above. A tiled square column is ≈0.6 × 0.6 m [seen: `commons_…_09/_10.jpg`].
  2. The **"flat" hall**, with ivory/cream tiles of the same size to ≈1.6 m [seen: `commons_…_03.jpg`].
- Floor: polished slabs, white with grey-brown veins (marble-look), ≈40 × 40 cm, in a stepped diagonal pattern [seen: `m24_2015_museum_05.jpg`, `commons_…_excursion.jpg`]. Not dated; "кафель на входе уже не родной" [fact: Фочкин].
- Counter: a long straight **bar counter ≈8–9 m** with a light wood-grain top ≈0.55–0.6 m deep at ≈1.05–1.1 m height. A padded dark-brown vinyl bumper roll runs along the front, and the end turns in a quarter circle. White wooden bar stools are ≈0.75 m high [seen: `m24_2015_museum_04/05/07.jpg`]. This counter is from the late 1980s or early 1990s [fact: Алексеева 2022 via Wikipedia]. For 1950s gameplay use instead a counter with a glass display case (prilavok-vitrina, ≈1.0 m high, 0.8 m deep) plus mechanical scales.
- Back wall: a run of built-in **mirror-backed glass-shelf display cases** with chrome tube uprights, on brown laminate lower cabinets (0.9 m). A white-painted cornice shelf at ≈2.4 m carries bottles. Each bay is ≈1.2 m wide × 1.5 m of glass [seen: `commons_…_05.jpg`, `m24_2015_museum_09.jpg`].
- Behind the counter is a narrow service corridor ≈1.2 m wide with a deep ceramic sink and a door to the back rooms [seen: `m24_2015_museum_09.jpg`, `commons_…_06.jpg`].
- Lighting: **opal glass cylinder pendants** ≈25 cm ⌀ × 30 cm on long cords with blue/orange ceramic cups, 4–6 over the counter [seen].
- Wall props: maroon glass boards «ПРЕЙСКУРАНТ», «ПРЕДЕЛЬНЫЕ НОРМЫ отпуска…», «ДЕНЬГИ ПОЛУЧАЕТ ПРОДАВЕЦ» (≈0.45 × 0.7 m; `m24_2015_museum_08.jpg`, `m24_2016_shop_07.jpg`); a shop electromechanical cash register (`m24_2016_shop_06.jpg`); dial scales; a large electric samovar hung with bagels; an aluminium red star with bulbs (≈1.4 m); food tins and tea packets (`m24_2016_shop_04/08.jpg`). Per svpressa, the original fittings were juice and water cones, glass dispensers and a mechanical cash register.
- Shop windows (street side): large single panes ≈2.2–2.4 m wide × 2.6–2.8 m high with a top-hung transom, sill ≈0.6 m above the pavement, white plaster surround. A cellar light-well grating sits under each window [seen: `m24_2015_houses_09.jpg`, `m24_2016_flat_04.jpg` from inside].
- Ceiling ≈3.6–3.8 m (the ground floor is taller than the flats) [est.: pendants on long cords; the column and arch proportions]. Plaster cornice as in the flats.
