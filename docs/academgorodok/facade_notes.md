# Академгородок Лаборатории № 2 — exterior facade notes

Sources: photos in `refs/exterior/` (see `manifest.json`), plus OpenStreetMap building
footprints (ways 27830839, 27830823, 27830844, fetched via Overpass 2026-10-24). File names
below are relative to `refs/exterior/`. **No architectural drawings were found openly online**
(see the end of this file). Everything below comes from photos and the OSM footprints. Each
estimate gives its reasoning; `(?)` marks uncertain items.

Photo dates matter. Most Commons photos are from 2011 (panoramio) and 2015 (Andreykor,
Flickr). `Moscow_Marshal_Novikov_street_3_2025-02_437` shows the 2020s capital repair
(Moskomekspertiza approved it, see mos.ru news 174745073): walls are repainted pale yellow
and window trims are white. The 1948 bird's-eye view (`pastvu_1983687`) shows the original
design. It may be a retouched photo or a photo of the model.

## 1. Common vocabulary and dimensions

* **Floor-to-floor height** is assumed to be ~3.75 m (3.4 m ceiling + slab).
  `Marshal_Novikov_str._1_2_`: double entrance doors ≈ 85 px wide, taken as ≈1.5 m, give
  ≈1.75 cm/px at the ground. Window-to-window vertical spacing there is ≈228 px ≈ 4.0 m,
  foreshortened looking up, so 3.7–3.8 m is consistent.
* **Plinth.** The ground floor is raised about 0.5–0.8 m. The plinth band is ≈0.6–0.7 m high.
  It is grey on Novikova 1 rear (`_1_3_`) and red-brown on Novikova 3 in 2011 (`panoramio_56`).
  There are 1–2 steps at the doors.
* **Eaves heights (estimates):**
  - 3-floor buildings: 0.7 + 3 × 3.75 ≈ **12 m**.
  - Novikova 1: 0.7 + 4 × 3.75 ≈ **15.7 m**.
* **Typical window** (Novikova 1 front, `_1_2_`):
  - Size ≈ **1.5 m wide × 1.9–2.0 m tall**.
  - Bay spacing ≈ **4.5 m** (260 px at 1.75 cm/px).
  - Sill height ≈ 0.9 m above the floor.
  - The opening is plain, with only a thin white reveal/frame band.
  - Glazing: 2 sashes with a transom, 4–6 panes. Today the windows are white PVC. In 1948
    they were wooden with a cross-mullion pattern.
  - Side buildings have smaller 2-light windows (≈1.3 × 1.8 m (?)).
  - Some windows are **paired with a turned colonnette** between them (wing faces on the
    courtyard side, `_1_4_`, `18996369890`).
  - Staircase windows are 3-light (≈1.9 m wide) and offset half a floor (`_1_3_`).
* **Cornice / eaves.** There are two systems:
  - **Novikova 1:** a projecting eaves (≈0.9–1.0 m) with a white plastered soffit and evenly
    spaced **white modillion brackets**, ≈0.25 m deep, about 0.6 m apart (≈12–14 per window
    bay). A thin fascia/gutter sits on top. These cornices were "reconstructed" according
    to Wikipedia.
  - **Side buildings:** a deep Tuscan/Italian timber eaves (≈1.0–1.2 m overhang) with
    **exposed rafter tails / shaped wooden brackets**, stained mid-brown, about 1.0–1.2 m
    apart, and a boarded soffit (`Maksimova_str._8_1_`, `_8_2_`, `panoramio_56`).
* **Roofs.** All are hipped and covered in painted sheet metal with standing seams. A low
  perimeter safety railing (~0.4 m) runs along the eaves.
  - **Novikova 1:** low pitch, ~20–25° (`_1_1_`, `_1_2_`), grey/galvanised.
  - **Side buildings:** ~25–30°, dark brown in 2015 (`8_1`) and dark grey in 2011
    (`panoramio_54`).
  - **Cross-gables.** In 1948 (`pastvu_1983687`) each loggia bay is crowned by a gable that
    cuts into the roof, and there are several dormers/vent hoods.
* **Chimneys.** Red brick stacks, about 0.9 × 0.6 m in plan, rising ~1.5–2 m above the roof.
  Each has a corbelled, **crenellated cap** (projecting courses with notches) and a small
  pyramidal sheet-metal hood on posts (`_1_3_`, `_1_4_`, `18996369890`).
  - Novikova 1: pairs placed symmetrically, with at least one either side of each gable.
  - Side buildings: 4–6 along the ridge.
* **Drainpipes.** Galvanised external downpipes, about 1 per 2–3 bays and at the corners.
* **Colours** (estimates; photos vary with exposure and filters):
  - Walls: ochre / pale yellow, sunlit ≈ `#E6D6A0` (a sample in good light read `#DCCEA3`),
    shade ≈ `#B3A577`. The 2025 repaint is a lighter lemon cream, ≈ `#EFE6B0`.
  - Trim (cornices, surrounds, columns, loggia parapets): off-white ≈ `#F1EEE4`.
  - Quoins: 2011 reddish-brown sandstone ≈ `#8C6656`; 2015 repainted grey-beige ≈ `#A39C8E`.
  - Central portal of Novikova 1: dark grey-black rusticated blocks ≈ `#2B2B2B`. In 2025 the
    side-building portals are brown, ≈ `#6B4A3A`.
  - Plinth: grey `#6F6F6C` (Novikova 1) or red-brown `#7A3E35` (side buildings, 2011).
  - Roofs: Novikova 1 grey `#9EA4A8`; side buildings dark brown `#4B3329` (2015).
  - Eaves timber: `#6A4A2E`.
  - Chimneys: brick `#9C5040` with grey metal caps.
  - Doors: dark brown/black `#1E1A18`. Window frames: white.
  - Gate scroll pylons: grey `#A3A1A0` (2015, `gates`), white in Flickr 2015 (filtered).

## 2. Novikova 1 (central, 4 floors, ground-floor Gastronom, now the museum)

### E facade (square side, main) — `Marshal_Novikov_str._1_2_`, `_1_1_`, `pastvu_1983687`
* Length 60.2 m. The building has 4 floors and no attic; the ground floor holds the shop.
* **Loggia bays.** The central bay is on the axis of the fountain and is ~5.5 m wide (315 px
  in `_1_2_`). A second identical bay stands to the S (left in `_1_1_`, crop confirms
  gable + oculus + loggias). By symmetry, and from the 1948 aerial (3 vertical loggia
  stacks), there is probably a third to the N; it is hidden by trees in all photos (?).
* **Bay rhythm (S→N, estimate):**
  2 windows | loggia bay | 3 (?4) windows | **central bay** | 3 (?4) windows | loggia bay | 2 windows.
  - Check: 10 × 4.5 m + 3 × 5.5 m ≈ 61.5 m, which is ≈60.2 m.
  - The count between bays is uncertain because of trees. `_1_1_` shows 3 visible plus room
    for a 4th.
* **Loggia bay composition** (bottom to top, `_1_2_`):
  1. **Ground floor.** The central entrance is set in a **dark rusticated portal** of large
     rough-faced blocks: jambs about 0.5 m wide and a stepped/fan-shaped top. The opening is
     ≈1.6 × 2.6 m. Above it **4 large carved consoles** (≈0.4 m wide, ≈0.8 m tall) carry the
     2nd-floor balcony slab.
  2. **2nd floor.** Enclosed loggia/balcony with a **solid white parapet**, ~1.0 m tall, the
     full bay width. It is flanked by **pilasters with ornamental (grotesque/candelabra)
     relief panels**. A 3-light window/door sits behind.
  3. **3rd floor.** Open loggia: **2 slender white columns + 2 end pilasters** with small
     capitals carry an entablature slab. There is a metal bar railing (~1.0 m) and a
     French door + side lights behind.
  4. **4th floor.** Balcony with a metal railing set before a **Serliana / Palladian window**:
     a central arched-top opening (lunette) with narrower flat-topped side lights, with
     pilasters between. The 4th-floor loggia also has 2 columns.
  5. **Gable.** A triangular gable, ~6 m wide, rises above the eaves line (apex about 3 m
     above the cornice). It has a white raking cornice and a **round oculus** (Ø ≈ 0.9 m)
     with a white moulded ring, flanked by **two stucco festoons/swags**. The eaves
     cornice stops against the bay's sides.
  - The bay projects only ≈0.3–0.5 m (?), so it reads as a shallow risalit.
* **Ground floor beside the bays.**
  - Shop/flat entrances: **double doors** (dark brown, glazed upper panels) in flat white
    architrave surrounds ≈0.2 m wide. Clear size ≈1.5 × 2.6 m. In `_1_2_` there is one
    entrance either side of the central bay, at ≈5 m from its edge.
  - **Shop windows:** large (~2.0 × 2.2 m), in white surrounds, with low sills (~0.6 m).
* **Other details.**
  - No rustication on the ground floor (plain plaster) except the dark portal.
  - **No string courses** are visible.
  - Upper windows have no pediments or keystones, only a thin white frame.
  - Some windows have low decorative railings (flower-box guards).
* **1948 (`pastvu_1983687`).** The roof was steeper and hipped, with cross-gables, and all
  loggias were open. The facade was in shadow, so detail is limited.

### N and S end facades — `Marshal_Novikov_str._1_gates` (end next to the N gate), `18563431703` (background)
* About 14.4 m wide, 4 floors.
* **2-storey polygonal bay window** (3-sided, glazed, white frames) on the ground and 2nd
  floors at the left (yard side). It has a cornice band on top and an **open balcony** on
  the 3rd floor with a white metal railing.
* **Arched side door**, ≈1.2 × 2.6 m, with a white arched surround, at the right (street)
  end of the ground floor.
* Middle: a small box projection with cornice and sill bands (enclosed loggia/WC (?)) on
  the 2nd–3rd floors at the right.
* 2–3 windows per floor plus small square vents (0.3 m).
* The opposite (S) end is assumed to mirror this (?). There is no photo.

### W facade (yard side) — `Marshal_Novikov_str._1_3_`
* Plain, with no decoration except the eaves modillions.
* Entrance section:
  - Door in a white flat surround with no canopy.
  - Staircase windows (3-light) stacked on the axis at mid-landing levels.
  - A **round oculus** (Ø ≈ 0.7 m glazing in a ~1.4 m white ring) under the eaves above.
* Flat windows to either side.
* The eaves line steps (part of the wall plane is set back).
* Balconies with metal railings on concrete slabs on the adjoining section (right).
* Number of entrances on the W side: unknown (?). The 1948 caption says 60 flats in 3
  houses; OSM gives 18 flats in Novikova 1.

## 3. Side buildings — Novikova 3 (N) and Maksimova 8 (S), mirror twins, 3 floors

### Street facade (Novikova 3 NE / Maksimova 8 SE) — `pastvu_1983687` (crop: M8), `Moscow_Marshal_Novikov_street_3_2025-02_437` (N3), `panoramio_54/56`, `Maksimova_str._8_1_`
* Length about 63 m. 3 floors plus the raised plinth.
* **Rhythm** from the far end to the gate corner, 1948 aerial of M8, ~15 window axes:
  2 windows | **gabled loggia bay over an entrance** | ~4 windows | **central entrance portal** (tall aedicule framing the door + the window above) | ~5 windows | **entrance portal / enclosed bay** | 2 windows | corner with quoins.
  - The 2025 photo of N3 shows the same: a gable over a brown rusticated portal with a
    loggia/balcony stack, 2–3 bays from the far (NW) corner, and a second gable further along.
  - Bay spacing ≈ 63 m / 15 ≈ 4.2 m (?).
* **Entrance portals** (`panoramio_58`, probably a side building, 2011; `2025` crop):
  - Jambs of **alternating rusticated blocks**, cream (2011) or brown (2025).
  - A moulded hood/cornice slab on top, about 2.2 m wide.
  - Door opening ≈1.3 × 2.4 m, metal door today.
  - Above: a window in a white architrave with "ears" (crossettes).
  - Portals with loggias also have **large scrolled consoles** under the balcony
    (`2025` crop, `18996369890`).
* **Windows.** Ground- and 2nd-floor windows have **white architraves with a straight hood
  cornice (сандрик)**; the 3rd floor has a plain frame (2025; also visible 2011 in
  `panoramio_54`).
* **Corner at the gate end** (`Maksimova_str._8_1_`, `panoramio_54/56`, `18563431703`):
  - **Rusticated quoin**: 10–11 courses of alternating long/short blocks with a
    vermiculated (worm-tracked, "frosted") face. Each course is ≈0.5 m tall, and the blocks
    are ≈0.9 m and ≈0.6 m long on each face. The quoin runs from the plinth to about the
    3rd-floor sill (~6 m tall).
  - It is capped by a small **pedestal/plinth with a cornice** (≈1.0 m wide, 0.35 m tall).
  - On that sits the **"1947" cartouche**: a shield/escutcheon about 0.6 × 0.8 m with a
    **5-point star** above the date "1947", topped by a ribbon/scroll bow. It is white, in
    relief, at 3rd-floor window level on the corner arris (it sits on the corner, facing
    diagonally).
  - The same cartouche is on **both** Novikova 3 (S/E corner, house-number plate "3") and
    Maksimova 8 (E corner, plate "8"). The two corners face each other across the
    Novikova 1 frontage, one on each gate.

### Gate-end facade (N3 SE end / M8 NE end, ~20 m) — `panoramio_56`, `Maksimova_str._8_1_`
* **4 window axes** per floor, irregularly spaced: a wider pier next to the quoin corner.
* One ground-floor window has a hood cornice and may be a former door (?).
* The **arched wicket** structure (see Gates) attaches at the yard end of this facade.

### Far-end facade (N3 NW end / M8 SW end) — `Maksimova_str._8_2_`, 2025 crop
* Corner bay element:
  - **Ground floor:** a large **arched window**, ≈1.8 m wide, with an arched white surround
    in a wall pier, next to a 3-part glazed bay.
  - **2nd floor:** a projecting **glazed bay** (enclosed loggia) on a cornice band, framed by
    pilasters.
  - **3rd floor:** an **open balcony** on top with a metal bar railing (brown).
* Plain 2-light windows on the rest of the facade.

### Courtyard facade (N3 SW / M8 NW) — `Moscow_Marshala_Novikova_Street_18996369890_` (N3), `Marshal_Novikov_str._1_4_` (probably M8)
* The end wings project ~4 m. Their side faces have **paired windows with a central turned
  colonnette** on every floor.
* The recessed centre has **stacked balconies** on the 2nd and 3rd floors, ~4.5 × 1.2 m. They
  carry **ornamental pierced/cast railings** (one has a solid panelled front) and sit on
  **large S-scroll stucco consoles**.
* Entrance doors are at ground level with no portal.
* Walls are plain, with the same timber eaves and chimneys.

## 4. Gates (two identical, N and S) — `Marshal_Novikov_str._1_gates`, `18563431703`, `Maksimova_str._8_1_` (right edge), `panoramio_56` (left edge)
Heritage listing: "пилоны двух ворот с калитками" (pylons of two gates with wickets).

* **Wicket (калитка) block.** A low wall-block attached to the side building's end. It is
  ≈4.5 m tall and ≈3.5–4 m long, ochre, with a white moulded cornice and a white band below
  it. It has a semicircular **arched passage** ≈1.8 m wide and ≈3 m to the crown, with a
  white surround. A small green (2011/2015) metal hipped roof covers it.
  - Estimate from `gates`: the arch is ~210 px wide against a car width of ~1.8 m ≈ 190 px.
* **Gate pylons.** Two free-standing **scroll-shaped (volute) pylons** in mirror pairs. Each
  is a thick slab ~0.5 m deep, ≈1.3 m wide at its widest and **≈2.5–3 m tall**:
  - a big volute curls at the top;
  - a stepped/"S" profile runs down the edge;
  - a smaller curl sits at the bottom, on a rectangular base block ~0.5 m tall.
  - Painted grey (2015) / white (filtered photo).
  - The clear opening between them is ≈3.5–4 m (car-width scaling in `gates`).
  - Height estimate: the right pylon is ≈2× the height of the yellow bollards beside it
    (bollards ~1.1–1.2 m).
* **Ironwork.** No gate leaves or ironwork survive in any 2011–2015 photo. Today the
  opening is open, with bollards. Wikipedia's "volutes like Italian neo-Renaissance" most
  likely describes the **scroll pylons themselves**, not ironwork (?).
  - Original iron leaves, if any, are unknown.
  - `pastvu_528948` (1950s) shows a tall iron gate with vertical bars and a lantern on a
    massive pylon. That gate most likely belongs to the neighbouring No. 2 ensemble and is
    **not** this gate.
* Layout across the ~14–15 m gap, side building → Novikova 1 (estimate):
  wicket block (~4 m) → left pylon → ~4 m drive → right pylon → ~2–3 m paved strip → Novikova 1
  end with the arched side door.
  - The order is confirmed by `gates` and `18563431703`. The exact spacing is not.

## 7. Biggest uncertainties
1. **Novikova 1 E facade window count.** The number of windows between the loggia bays is 3
   or 4, and the existence of a 3rd (N) loggia bay is inferred from symmetry and the 1948
   aerial. Trees hide it in all photos.
2. **Novikova 1 W/yard facade.** Only one entrance section is photographed. The number of
   entrances and the balcony positions are unknown.
3. **Side-building street facades.** The bay count (~15) and portal positions come from the
   1948 aerial, which is low-resolution and possibly a model photo, plus one oblique 2025
   photo. There is no straight-on photo.
4. **Original gate ironwork.** There is no evidence of what filled the openings.
5. **Roof pitch changes.** Novikova 1's roof looks flatter today than in 1948. The cornices
   were reconstructed, so the profiles may not be original.
6. **Colours.** All are estimated from consumer photos with varying exposure. Two photos
   are heavily colour-filtered (Flickr). The 2025 repaint gives the "historical" scheme
   the city claims to have restored.

## 8. Archive drawings found later (refs/interior/, photographed in m24.ru 2015; reference only)

* `m24_2015_houses_06.jpg`: 1946 street elevation of a 3-storey side house, developed with its gate end:

  - street facade: ground-floor windows with **hood cornices and sill brackets**; **portals** of rusticated
    blocks around **round-arched doors**; over each portal a **balcony on consoles** at the 2nd floor, a
    window with a **triangular pediment** behind it, hooded windows either side; plain 3rd-floor windows;
  - **small ornamented gables** (with swags) on the eaves over the portals; hipped roof, tall chimneys.
  - The window step on the drawing is denser than on the 1948 aerial; the model uses 2-5-5-2 windows between

  the same portal (brown rustication, consoles, balcony) under a small gable, built.
* Proportions of Novikova 1's square facade re-measured on the panorama `commons_Marshal_Novikov_str._1_1_.jpg`:
  **4 windows between the loggia bays** (not 3), window step ≈4.05 m, bay ≈5.4 m, side bays at ±20.85 m,
  floor to floor ≈3.8 m, windows ≈1.4 × 1.9 m.
