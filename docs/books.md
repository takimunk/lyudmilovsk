# The library's classics

The town library (P-LIBRARY) lends the world's classics: about 390 works in `tools/books/canon.txt` (antiquity, Japan,
Russia, England, the Americas, France, Germany and elsewhere), each in Russian, English and Japanese where an open text
exists. The hero borrows up to five at the lending desk, carries them as `library_book` in his pocket and reads them
anywhere. The best place is a park bench.

## Where the texts come from

| Language | Source | How it is found |
|---|---|---|
| en | Project Gutenberg (the `gutenberg.pglaf.org` mirror; `pg_catalog.csv.gz`) | title and author match, the Best Books / Classics shelves first; `pins.json` picks editions by hand (a play inside a collection, a different English title, a work in volumes) |
| ja | Aozora Bunko (its `list_person_all_extended_utf8.zip` CSV) | title prefix and author match, 作品著作権フラグ なし, the translator's death year known; a work in parts takes every part by the same translator |
| ru | Russian Wikisource (`index.php` only: `action=raw` for the header, `action=render` for the text) | "Title (Surname)" pages and the search; the edition subpages ВТ:Ё > ВТ > СО > ДО; translated works need a named translator |

**The licence rule:** every creator of a text (author, translator, editor of the version used) died in 1955 or
earlier. The script checks it from the sources' own death years; a text whose translator's death year is unknown is
skipped unless `pins.json` names the translation (an anonymous one carries its year). Every text keeps its source URL,
creators and licence in `catalog.json` and `books.json`, and the reader prints them on the book's last page.

## Files

- `tools/books/canon.txt`: `id|orig lang|year|author en|title en|author ru|title ru|author ja|title ja`.
- `tools/books/pins.json`: editions picked by hand.
- `tools/books/catalog.json` (committed, generated by `resolve`): which text of each work, per language.
- `unreal/Lyudmilovsk/Content/Books/<lang>/<id>.txt` + `books.json` (**not in git or LFS**, made by `fetch` on each
  machine that builds the game, staged as plain files by `DirectoriesToAlwaysStageAsNonUFS=(Path="Books")`).
  Without them the catalogue is empty and the log says to run the fetch.

```
python3 tools/books/fetch_books.py resolve [ids] [--lang ru,en,ja]   # canon -> catalog.json (slow: 1 request/s per host)
python3 tools/books/fetch_books.py fetch [ids]                        # catalog -> Content/Books (cached in tools/books/.cache)
python3 tools/books/fetch_books.py report
```

Text format: UTF-8, paragraphs separated by a blank line, a verse paragraph keeps its line breaks, `# ` starts a
heading (chapters, acts; the reader's contents list and running heads come from them). Gutenberg's title pages,
contents lists and `[Illustration]` notes are dropped; Aozora's ruby and notes are stripped.

## In the game

- **Lending desk** (`P-LIBRARY/lending_counter_2`, act `library_classics` with `"open": "books"` in
  `tools/city/content/x_p_library.json`; needs the librarian at work): the catalogue. Language filter, search, borrow,
  give back, read. At most `ULyudBooks::MaxBorrowed` (5) on loan.
- **Pocket:** each book on loan is one `library_book` (inventory "Read" opens it; one book opens straight away, several
  open his shelf).
- **Benches** (`ULyudBenchSit`): near a free seat of a public "bench" spot, `E — sit on the bench`; seated with a book,
  `E — read`. The seat is reserved in `ULyudPublicSpotsSubsystem`, so townsfolk don't sit on him. Any step or Space gets
  him up.
- **The reader** (`SLyudBookReader`): two pages on a wide window, one on a narrow one; laid out on a worker
  (`Private/Books/BookLayout.h`, engine-free, kinsoku for Japanese). D / Right / PageDown / Space / E / click on the
  right page / wheel: forward; A / Left / PageUp: back; Home: first page; Q: contents (W/S scroll); + / -: type size;
  Esc or Tab: close. The game doesn't pause: each page read moves the clock on by its reading time (1000 characters a
  minute, 450 for Japanese, at most 5 game minutes a turn). The bookmark and "read to the end" are saved ("Books").
- Quests can listen to `borrow` and `read_book` notifications (`ULyudQuests::Notify`).

## Developer mode and console

F7 → Books group: go to the lending desk, read a book (borrows one if he has none), sit on the nearest bench, return
everything. Teleport `books.park_bench`. Console: `lyud.Books list [lang] | borrow <key> | return <key> | read [key] |
desk | mine | turn <n> | close | reset | stats`, `lyud.Bench.Sit [metres]`.

## Tests

`tools/booksim/run.sh`: the layout engine without Unreal (line breaking, kinsoku, headings, verse, page lookup, the
real books when Content/Books exists). `tools/citysim` covers the desk's act.
