## Found, not fixed (need a decision or a look in the game)

- **Rounds:** a round under way is not saved (save and load mid-round: no round and no pay for the doors done); the
  deadline is an hour of the day, so a round forced late in the evening never ends by itself.
- **Friends:** an invitation is marked before its letter is posted (if posting fails the hero is never told); a bench
  friend whose walk times out blocks the others for that sit; a treat is free if the inventory opens on the frame the
  animal finishes eating.
- **Kites:** a pooled town kite keeps its first colours; with hand bones missing the kite can only be put away through
  the inventory; each kite in the air sweeps every frame (wants a measurement).
- **Garden:** the neighbour's body is respawned every second if it cannot be made; static raw pointers to engine meshes
  are kept across world reloads (the same pattern in about 11 places: wants one decision).
- **Yard games and smooth motion:** with lyud.Anim.Smooth on, a rope jump or a hop is flattened by the height spring and
  a running child is drawn behind its model position; a pooled bird body eases from the previous bird's yaw.
- **Books and music:** a save made before tonight, loaded after playing in the same process, keeps the newer fields of
  the earlier session; the radio re-lays its timeline four times a second while heard; book credits keep the language
  they were loaded in.
- **Paper (dev only):** printing days ahead keeps those issues, which then lack the real day's news.
- **Chess:** the machine's move is searched in one frame (about a tenth of a second at level 3).
- **Growth of saved state (slow):** letters and cards, cup results, lab bookings and after-storm triggers are never pruned.
