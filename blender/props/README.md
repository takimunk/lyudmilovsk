# Reusable props

Generated by the scripts in this folder (`blender -b -P blender/props/build_<group>.py -- [names] [--no-render]`), shared
helpers in `propslib.py`. Each prop is exported to `export/<name>.glb` (+ `<name>.blend` here); `export/manifest.json` has
the bounding boxes. Textures: `textures/props/` (fal.ai, `tools/fal_prop_textures.py`), embedded into the glb as JPEG.
Dimensions are metres in **Blender axes** (X right, Y depth, Z up); glTF import turns this into Y-up as for the corridor.

Groups: `build_keyboards.py`, `build_computers.py` (monitors, TVs, Macs, PC cases, laptops, mice), `build_consoles.py`
(consoles and pads), `build_desks.py` (7 kinds x 4 widths), `build_chairs.py`.

## Pivot conventions (same as `build_scene01_corridor.py`)

* **devices** (keyboards, computers, monitors, consoles, pads, mice): origin on the table top under the middle of the
  device, the screen/front faces **-Y** (toward the chair), +X right, +Z up. A screen is its own mesh `<prop>_Screen`
  with the material `Screen_Off`, UV 0..1 with V down (a normal image), so the scene can swap in its `Screen_On_*` material.
* **desks**: origin in the middle of the top surface (`z = 0` is the top), floor at `z = -0.75`, chair side `-Y`, wall `+Y`.
  Depth 0.80 m. Widths 1.30 / 1.45 / 1.60 / 1.75 m (`desk_<kind>_<cm>`); scale Z for other heights.
* **chairs**: origin on the floor under the post, the sitter faces **+Y**, backrest at -Y, seat top ~0.47 m.
* Lights: emissive materials named `LED_*` (small lamps), no real light objects in the files.
