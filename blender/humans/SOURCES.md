# Sources and licences of the human assets

The humans are generated by `build_humans.py` from MakeHuman data through MPFB2. No third-party
binary is committed: `fetch_assets.sh` downloads everything, the build writes the .blend/.glb locally.

## Tools and base data

| What | Where | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| MPFB2 v2.0.18 (MakeHuman for Blender), program code | https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2 | GPL-3.0 (tool only, not shipped) |
| MPFB2 bundled assets: base mesh, modelling targets, rigs (`game_engine`, `default`), weights | same repo, `src/mpfb/data` | CC0 1.0 |
| Output of MPFB (meshes, exports, renders) | MakeHuman team statement, LICENSE.md section D | no claim, "your data" |
| makehuman_system_assets (skins, eyes, eyebrows, eyelashes, teeth, tongue, system hair/shoes) | https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/makehuman_system_assets.html | CC0 1.0 |
| faceunits01 (52 ARKit face shape targets, by Mika Suominen), visemes01 | https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/faceunits01.html | CC0 1.0 (per-target licence in the pack index) |

Skeleton = MPFB `game_engine` rig (UE mannequin bone names) + `jaw`, `eye_l`, `eye_r` bones and
weights taken from MPFB's `default` rig (both CC0).

## Animation clips

The shared human clips (`/Game/Characters/Humans/Anims/A_Human_*`) are retargeted by `retarget_clips.py` from
`clips.json`; the downloads are fetched by `fetch_clips.sh` and never committed (only our retargeted clips are).

| Clips | Source | Licence | Author |
|---|---|---|---|
| Walk (Walk_Loop), StandIdle (Idle_Loop), Talk (Idle_Talking_Loop), SitDown (Sitting_Enter), SitIdle (Sitting_Idle_Loop), SitTalk (Sitting_Talking_Loop), StandUp (Sitting_Exit) | Universal Animation Library, Standard: https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html (OpenGameArt: https://opengameart.org/content/universal-animation-library) | CC0 1.0 | Quaternius |
| Nod (Yes), ShakeHead (Idle_No_Loop), FoldArms (Idle_FoldArms_Loop), Phone (Idle_TalkingPhone_Loop) | Universal Animation Library 2, Standard: https://opengameart.org/content/universal-animation-library-2 | CC0 1.0 | Quaternius |
| LieDown, Sleep, GetUp (on the side) | our own (`sleep_clips.py`) | CC BY 4.0 (this repo) | Lyudmilovsk |
| Eat, Read | our own (`life_anims.py`), carried over to the generated bodies by `retarget_clips.py` | CC BY 4.0 (this repo) | Lyudmilovsk |
| each person's seated computer loops (`A_<Name>_Sit_*`) | our own (`seat_anims.py`), baked by `bake_own_clips.py`, carried over by `retarget_clips.py --own` | CC BY 4.0 (this repo) | Lyudmilovsk |

CC0 asks for nothing; Quaternius is credited here anyway. The lip-sync curves (`tools/lipsync.py`) are computed
from our own voice lines.

## Our own additions (CC BY 4.0 with the rest of this repo's assets)

Garment re-tailoring (oversized hoodie, wide jeans, baggy tees), recolours, the striped tie texture,
the clerk's black sleeve covers (cut from the shirt mesh), the streamer's headset with boom mic, and
the seated and standing animation loops, the owner's fluffed-out hair, the gamepad are generated by the scripts in this folder.
