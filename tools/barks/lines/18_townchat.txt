## ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
## A talk with the hero (docs/town_talk.md "A talk"): E on a townsperson who is free for a word opens a page with their
## greeting, two to four things to ask about, now and then a question back, and a goodbye. These are their lines:
## sets with on = tc_*, picked like any other line of theirs (job, age, the hour, the weather, the bond, the rumours).
## The hero's side (his questions and answers), the events and which activity or like belongs to which event:
## tools/townchat/content.py; python3 tools/townchat/build_town_chat.py checks this file against it.
## {0}: the nickname a friend gave him (only in sets with bond = friend close). {1}: a rumour's detail (only on =
## tc_rumour with heard_of). {2}..{7}: the given name of whoever the line names, in the six cases (only tc_friend,
## tc_kin_*); the same numbers in ru, en and ja (English and Japanese get the plain name for each).
## Russian: the speaker never speaks of themselves in the past tense (no gender); the hero is a boy, they say «ты».
## A page shows one line: ru and en up to 100 characters, ja up to 48 (tools/barksim checks).
## ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
