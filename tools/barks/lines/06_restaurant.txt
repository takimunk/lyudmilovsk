## The restaurant «Янтарь»: the waiter (events of the hero's visit), the cook in the kitchen, the guests at their tables
## Events (on = ...): rest_sit (the hero sat down at a table), rest_carry (the waiter takes a tray from the pass; the cook
## says his line then), rest_serve (a dish set down on the hero's table), rest_bill (the bill brought), rest_bye (the
## hero got up after eating).
## ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

on = rest_sit

say town.waiter_sit.3 | Хороший столик. Отсюда закат лучше всего видно. | A good table. You get the best view of the sunset from here. | いいお席ですね。ここから夕日がいちばんよく見えます。
