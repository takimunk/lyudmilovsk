
## ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
## The holidays' own voices (docs/festivals.md "Every holiday's voices"): on each holiday of the calendar an adult says
## what they are bringing, a nervy one complains, a child tells the day's game, an elder remembers how it used to be.
## One line a set, said only on that holiday. Russian: the speaker's own words never agree in gender (no "я принёс").
