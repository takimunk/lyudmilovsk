## ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
## The days (docs/town_life.md "The days", Private/People/PeopleDays.cpp): what today is for somebody (today = cold,
## sleepy, birthday, payday, pension, guest), the errands of the week (the Saturday market, the library's books, the
## pension at the office), the day's plans (tea at a friend's, Sunday at the grandparents', a couple's stroll, chess on
## a bench), and two who know each other on the way (today = street_meet, on = street_nod, on = door_hold).
## No first-person past tense here: the lines are said by men and women alike.
## ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
