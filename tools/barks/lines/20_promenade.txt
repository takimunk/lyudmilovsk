## ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
## The warm season's evenings on Sun Square and the promenade (docs/promenade.md): the summer dances by the stage
## (prom_dance, prom_dance_watch), the brass band on Sunday afternoons (band_play, band_listen, band_kids), the evening
## walk to the pier and back and the benches at sunset (prom_evening_walk, prom_sunset_bench), the couples' strolls,
## the lighthouse coming on, the photographer's best light. Russian: the speaker's own words never agree in gender
## unless the set says sex; the hero is a teenage boy, and only girls of his age ask him.

## ---- the brass band on Sunday afternoon

[pev_band_play]
doing = band_play
weight = 4
say town.pev_band_play.1 | Раз, два… «Амурские волны», с затакта. Тромбоны, не спешим. | One, two… "Waves of the Amur", from the upbeat. Trombones, don't rush. | ワン、ツー…「アムールの波」、アウフタクトから。トロンボーン、急がないで。
say town.pev_band_play.2 | Мундштук на солнце нагрелся, губы обжёг. Зато звук золотой. | The mouthpiece got hot in the sun and burnt my lips. But the sound's golden. | マウスピースが日に焼けて唇をやけどした。そのかわり音は金色。

## ---- the evening promenade

[pev_walk]

weight = 4

say town.pev_walk.2 | Вечером на набережной все друг с другом здороваются. Даже те, кто днём не здоровается. | In the evening on the promenade everybody says hello. Even those who don't by day. | 夕方の遊歩道では、みんなあいさつする。昼間はしない人まで。

say town.pev_walk.4 | Пахнет морем и жареными семечками. Это и есть лето. | It smells of the sea and of roasted sunflower seeds. That's summer for you. | 海と炒ったひまわりの種の匂い。これが夏ってもの。
