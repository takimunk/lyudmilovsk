## ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
## The talk of the town about the hero (backlog item 22, docs/town_rumours.md; kinds and rules: tools/rumours/rumours.py).
## heard_of = <kind>: the speaker heard of something he did (saw it, or heard it from their household, workmates or a
## friend: a far terrace a day later) and would bring it up now. At most one such remark per resident a day, each rumour
## once per resident, and the game keeps a long pause between any two (calm and rare). {1}: the rumour's detail (the cup,
## the regular beaten, the bird, the fish, the tune, the job as a place); {0} in bond sets: the friend's nickname for him.
## Russian: the speaker never speaks of themselves in the past tense (no gender); the hero is a boy.
## ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
