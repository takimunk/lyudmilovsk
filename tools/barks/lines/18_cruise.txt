## ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
## The weekend cruise of the motor ship «Полдень» (docs/ship.md; tools/ship/cruise_people.py): the townsfolk who
## sail this weekend (job = cruise_passenger: their twins aboard, and their town records waiting for the launch at
## the pier head). The crew and the tourists are talked to with E and say no lines of these. The islands by the
## programme: the Stone Brothers on Friday evening, Bird Island and the Far Light on Saturday, the Seal Rocks on Sunday.
## Russian: the speaker's own words never agree in gender. Nobody offers the hero a drink: he is a teenager.
