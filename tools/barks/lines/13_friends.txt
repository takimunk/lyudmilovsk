## Friends among the townsfolk (backlog item 12; the words of the feature: tools/friends/content.py).
## bond = friendly | friend | close: how close the speaker is to the hero (absent: anyone). {0} is the nickname the
## friend gave him (friends.nick.*), only in sets with bond = friend close. Greetings (to = hero), a wave from afar
## (on = friend_wave), the friend's day told on a bench (on = friend_bench), at their family's weekend outing
## (on = friend_outing), his postcard came (on = friend_card), his present remembered (on = friend_gift).
## Russian: the speaker's own words never agree in gender (no past tense about themselves); the hero is a boy.
